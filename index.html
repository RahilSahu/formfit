      Why forms reject your upload

      • Too heavy: a phone photo is 2 to 5 MB; the form may allow 50 KB. It rejects anything even 1 KB over.
      • Too light: most forms set a minimum as well (for example, 20 to 50 KB). Over-compressed files are rejected as too small.
      • Wrong shape: the notice fixes exact pixels (a bank exam photo is 200 x 230 px), not just size.
      • Wrong format: PNG or iPhone HEIC where only JPG is accepted.

      FormFit fixes all four in one pass, on your own device. Cyber cafes charge for this and keep copies of your documents on shared computers; random compressor sites upload your Aadhaar and marksheets to unknown servers. Here, nothing is uploaded at all.

      Presets reflect specs commonly published in recent exam notices. Notices change between cycles, so always check the numbers in your own notice and use Custom when they differ.
